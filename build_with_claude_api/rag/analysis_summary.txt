EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
-----------------
This comprehensive analysis of 500 streaming service users identified the
major drivers of customer churn (38.6% overall rate) and provides actionable
recommendations to reduce churn by up to 35%.

MAJOR CHURN DRIVERS (Ranked by Importance)
-------------------------------------------

1. ⭐ TOTAL VIEWING HOURS - 26.3% Importance (PRIMARY DRIVER)
   • Churned users: 66.6 hours/month vs Retained: 83.2 hours/month (-20%)
   • Users with <52.4 hours/month: 54.4% churn rate
   • Users with >99.9 hours/month: 22.4% churn rate
   • RISK MULTIPLIER: 2.43x for low-engagement users

2. ⭐ AVERAGE SESSION DURATION - 17.6% Importance
   • Churned users: 49.4 min/session vs Retained: 57.8 min/session (-14.4%)
   • Shorter sessions indicate difficulty finding engaging content
   • Suggests need for improved content discovery

3. ⭐ UNIQUE TITLES WATCHED - 16.1% Importance
   • Churned users: 19.5 titles/month vs Retained: 23.7 titles/month (-18%)
   • Users watching ≤16 titles: 54.3% churn rate
   • Users watching >16 titles: 32.5% churn rate
   • RISK MULTIPLIER: 1.67x for limited content exploration

4. CUSTOMER SERVICE INTERACTIONS - 13.7% Importance
   • Churned users: 3.2 interactions/year vs Retained: 2.5 (+27.7%)
   • Users with ≥4 interactions: 59.1% churn rate
   • Users with <4 interactions: 31.6% churn rate
   • RISK MULTIPLIER: 1.87x for high support needs

5. SUBSCRIPTION TIER & GENRE - Combined 13.1% Importance
   • Basic tier: 43.5% churn | Standard: 39.5% | Premium: 24.1%
   • Horror fans: 52.3% churn | Thriller: 48.3% | Documentary: 25.9%

CRITICAL FINDINGS
-----------------

HIGH-RISK SEGMENT IDENTIFIED:
• 218 users with BOTH low engagement AND high CS interactions
• Expected churn rate: 49.5% (vs 24.6% for normal users)
• This segment represents 43.6% of all users - IMMEDIATE ACTION NEEDED

ENGAGEMENT IS EVERYTHING:
The analysis reveals that low engagement (fewer hours, shorter sessions, less
content diversity) is the PRIMARY predictor of churn. This is GOOD NEWS because
engagement can be improved through:
  ✓ Better content recommendations
  ✓ Improved user interface/experience  
  ✓ Personalized content discovery
  ✓ Proactive engagement campaigns

ACTIONABLE RECOMMENDATIONS
---------------------------

TIER 1 - IMMEDIATE (0-30 Days):
• Deploy retention campaign for users with <60 viewing hours/month
• Flag users with 3+ CS interactions for proactive outreach
• Send personalized recommendations to Horror/Thriller fans
• Offer Premium trial upgrades to at-risk Basic tier users

TIER 2 - SHORT-TERM (30-90 Days):
• Redesign content discovery algorithm to promote diversity
• Create engagement programs for users watching <16 unique titles
• Analyze root causes of CS interactions (technical, billing, content)
• Develop genre-specific retention strategies

TIER 3 - LONG-TERM (90+ Days):
• Expand content library in high-churn genres (Horror, Thriller)
• Redesign onboarding to encourage content exploration
• Implement predictive churn model for early intervention
• Create compelling Premium tier value propositions
